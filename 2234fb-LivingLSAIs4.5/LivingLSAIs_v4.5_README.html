⭐ Why bother with an AI NPC mod?

Vanilla pedestrians have maybe forty voice lines and zero memory of you. Here is what actually changes when every person on the street can think, remember, and answer.

🎤

Say literally anything

No dialogue wheel, no fixed options. Talk with your actual voice or type it. Ask for directions, start an argument, negotiate a price, apologise, or lie — they respond to what you really said.

🧠

They remember you

Names, favours, insults, debts and grudges persist between sessions. Come back a week later and the guy you shot at still has something to say about it.

🗣️

Your reputation travels

Gossip spreads to nearby pedestrians and is written into their memory. Strangers can already know what you did two blocks away.

🎬

Stories start without you

The Episode director spawns real incidents between pedestrians — arguments, accusations, lost property, deals gone wrong — and you can walk into the middle of one.

🚕

They actually do things

Over a hundred action tags: drive you somewhere, follow you, fight beside you, call the cops, hand you money, race you, rob you, or walk off in disgust.

👁️

They can see the screen

Multimodal vision means an NPC can react to the burning car behind you, the gun in your hand, or the fact that it is raining.

📻

The city reports on you

Police dispatch names your crime and your street. Weazel News runs a bulletin. A helicopter calls your position. All of it AI-generated from what you actually did.

🎭

Forty personalities

From polite to psychopath to drunk desi uncle. Every NPC pulls a personality, a job, an age and a mood, so nobody sounds like the last person.

🆓

Free, forever

No paywall, no premium tier, no bundled key farming your requests. You bring your own free Gemini key and that is the whole cost.

🐛 The v4.4 Bug Hunt — 26 Kills

Each of these has a regression test that fails on the old code first, then passes on the new. If a fix cannot prove the bug existed, it is not a fix — it is a guess.

💸 NPC gives money, balance never changes

"He said he paid me. Nothing arrived."

Three faults stacked. The pickup hash 0xFE3CE0F6 was not a real hash. The fallback path swallowed its own exception so it never ran. And SHVDN's Player.Money setter has a default: return; — it silently writes nothing on any model that is not Michael, Franklin or Trevor.

🎭 "Yesterday Frankie, today Mario"

The same face had a new name every session.

Identity was seeded from new Random(npc.Handle). A handle is a recycled pool slot, not a person — the engine hands the same number to a completely different ped minutes later.

🧠 Memory never loaded

Saved NPCs greeted you like a stranger.

Files were keyed {model}_{nameSlug}.json. The moment the name changed, the file could never be found again.

🗣️ Rumours never spread

Gossip system existed, did nothing.

HasSavedMemoryFor tested for a {modelHash}.json filename that no longer existed after the memory rewrite, so every single ped was skipped.

📻 FlyLo FM never worked

One station simply refused to tune.

The code sent RADIO_14_SAMPLED. The real station name is RADIO_14_DANCE_02.

🏍️ Bike lifts refused

"Ride?" on a motorcycle always failed.

A collapsed ternary — (num6 == 8 ? 1 : (num6 == 13 ? 1 : 0)) == 0 — explicitly rejected vehicle class 8, which is Motorcycle.

😐 New moods never appeared

106 moods in the pool, the same dozen in play.

The savage/polite index lists were hardcoded with a maximum index of 59. Everything added after that was unreachable.

💥 Crash 0xc0000005 in the shop routine

Hard CTD roughly 30s into a shop scene.

The face-hold watchdog's own comment claimed "scene ownership always wins" — it never checked _sceneActive. One ped was owned by three systems at once and CLEAR_PED_TASKS fired every 900ms for thirty seconds, tearing up the scene's task tree while it ran. The test reproduced 34 destructive clears on the old code and 0 on the new.

🩲 "Everyone thinks I'm naked"

Fully suited Michael, announced to the city as nude.

Clothing truth read drawable 0 on components 11 and 4 and called it undressed. That is true on a freemode body — and false on every protagonist. Michael's stock outfit is 0/0. Nudity is now only inferred on models where drawable 0 provably is the bare body.

🖥️ Chatbox vanished after 2-3 seconds

120 force-releases in 40 seconds of log.

The 45-second panel watchdog called UiClearStage() to recover — and that method never reset the timestamp it is judged by. Anything that re-opened the panel matched the stale timestamp instantly, so the watchdog killed it again every frame.

📝 Chatbox truncated past ~380 characters

Long replies just stopped mid-word.

CELL_EMAIL_BCON has exactly four slots of 99 characters — a hard ceiling of 396. The push loop added a fifth component, which malforms the command, and the engine drew nothing at all.

🚀 LEANONCAR launched cars into orbit

Ask someone to lean on your car, watch it fly.

The ped was teleported to a point inside the car's collision shell. The physics engine resolved the overlap the only way it can — with a separating impulse.

🔇 Radio break played 85 chunks of silence

Break ran, nothing audible.

The break never claimed the audio lane, so gain stayed at 0.0 for the whole transmission.

🎵 Music returned mid-break

Station cut back in over the adverts.

The duck was held for the audio that had just played (3.8s) — but the next stage takes 15-30s to generate. The hold expired in the gap.

🔊 Music died for an entire chase

51 seconds of silence, and Q stopped opening the radio wheel.

Every dispatch call pushed the duck ceiling forward by 20s. During a pursuit they fire faster than that, so the ceiling was shoved forward forever. The wheel died with it, because it cannot open on a vehicle whose radio is disabled. There is now an absolute 25-second cap, whatever stacks up behind it.

📰 NPCs never remembered the news

Bulletin aired, city learned nothing.

SeedCityAwareness() was wired to the standalone bulletin only. The radio-break news stage never called it — four breaks aired in one session and not a single awareness seed was written.

🔁 The same advert twice in a row

"Tired of waiting for GTA Six?" — again.

Every cache refill used an identical prompt, so it found the same trend and wrote the same joke, with no idea what was already cached. Now: a banned list of existing copy, a rotating angle and brand per batch, and a similarity check that rejects near-duplicates on the way in.

🌍 Ads always in English

Hindi or Portuguese selected — adverts still English.

Three code paths, only two carried the language lock. The one that was missing it was the one that writes the cached copy. It authored in English, and the reader was then told to "read this word for word" — which beats a language lock aimed at the reader.

💀 "No response" death spiral

Once it broke, it stayed broken.

closeGeminiSession() ran unconditionally on every start, killing sockets that were still in setup.

🛒 Los Santos Customs menu locked up

Drive into LSC during a break, lose control.

The N skip key used SuppressKeyPress, starving the shop menu of the same input.

⌨️ Dismiss key ignored

Your rebound key did nothing.

It was read at the bottom of the key handler, behind sixteen early returns.

🚫 "Let me look that up" on air

The anchor narrated their own research.

Nothing forbade it. There is now an explicit ban list on every broadcast prompt.

🚁 "AIR UNIT" on an advert

Commercials wore a police badge.

The avatar emblem block had no branch for the ad skin, so it fell through to the helicopter badge.

🗨️ Shop scenes drifted off-topic

"You got any food?" → a lecture about the weather.

Routines inherited the player's last direct line. Each routine now states its single purpose and bans small talk outright.

🚶 Traffic drivers hijacked into scenes

A moving car's driver got out mid-road.

No driver guard existed and the scene called TASK_LEAVE_ANY_VEHICLE on whoever it picked.

📵 Break self-killed its own audio

Break aborted one second after starting.

Disabling the vehicle radio makes GET_PLAYER_RADIO_STATION_NAME return RADIO_36_AUDIOPLAYER. The station-change guard read that as the player switching station, and aborted the break it had just started.

🧠 Memory v3 — Grudges, Debts & Gossip

NPC memory is no longer a flat friendship score. Individual events carry weight, decay at their own speed, and can travel to other people's memory files.

⏳

Exponential grudge decay

Not everything is forgiven at the same rate. Shooting at someone stays in their head for roughly two weeks. A plain insult fades in about a day. Everything in between scales smoothly.

SHOT_AT_ME weight 9 → 4 after 14 days INSULTED_ME weight 2 → 0 after 3 days I_SHOVED_THEM weight -2 "you shoved them"
🗣️

Gossip that actually spreads

When something notable happens it propagates outward to nearby pedestrians and is written directly into their memory files. Walk back an hour later and a total stranger may already know.

  • Radius grows with each hop
  • Up to 14 peds per event
  • 15 minute freshness window

Debts are tracked separately

Money owed in either direction is stored as its own record, so an NPC can be friendly with you and still want their two hundred bucks back.

Fully backward compatible

Old v2 memory files load without conversion. Nothing you already built with an NPC is lost.

Two NPCs, Three NPCs, One Shared World

Each participant gets an identity, voice, turn, transcript, physical state, and responsibility. The player can interrupt the scene without destroying the current NPC audio.

👥

Two-NPC Interaction

NPC A walks to NPC B, asks the real requested question, hears an independent answer, remembers it, performs justified physical actions, and can report back.

👥👤

Three-NPC Interaction

NPC A, B, and C share a chronological scene with fair round-robin turns, optional targeted routing, language locking, and duplicate-participant protection.

01 • DEALNPC may accept, refuse, negotiate, request money, or take a weapon.
02 • LOCKThe selected numbered target remains the same real entity.
03 • ASKNPC A physically approaches and makes the required demand/question.
04 • REACTTarget answers independently and may comply, cower, cover, flee, dance, or resist.
05 • RESOLVEThe executor advances conditional actions and the scene reaches an outcome.

Player interruption

During the linked scene, speak through the microphone: “Ask again,” “Stop aiming,” “Tell both,” “Let her go,” “Keep going,” or “End this.” Your final transcript enters the shared scene without cancelling the active speaker’s PCM.

Stop action ≠ stop conversation

“Stop dancing” changes the physical state and continues dialogue. “End the conversation” closes the linked scene. “No stopping / keep going” explicitly forbids stop tags for that turn.

Auto NPC Convo — Full Brief Explanation

This system should be explained as one of the most important “alive world” features preserved and expanded in v4.3.

👀

Staring Reactions

Keep looking at someone too long and they may notice, question you, warn you, or start talking first.

🧍

Personal Space Pressure

Standing too close can trigger discomfort, hostility, mockery, or defensive lines.

💥

Physical Bump Reactions

If you physically bump into them, some NPCs now answer back instead of silently eating the collision.

🚗

Vehicle & Horn Tension

Street behavior can react to vehicle pressure, horn harassment, and nearby driving annoyance.

📣

Passerby Flavor Lines

Some reactions are simply ambient one-liners to make the city feel socially alive, not just combat-ready.

🧹

Cooldowns & Cleanup

When chaos happens

Troubleshooting

Keep this practical. Users need answers, not philosophy.

NPCs are not speaking correctly

Check your Gemini setup first. Then read LLAIsession.log and LLAIDEBUG.log for provider, bridge, or image-send clues.

Vision feels wrong or stale

Use the vision diagnostics. The logs now show capture, queue, send, and bridge status information so you can see whether a frame was actually delivered.

Bridge not working

Check that the bridge files exist in the expected path and review bridge_audio.log plus bridge_fatal.log.

Quota exceeded / 429 errors in the log

That is your Google key hitting its free-tier limit, not a mod bug. Ambient scenario generation falls back to the cached library automatically and live conversations keep working. Wait for the quota window to reset or use another key.

Too many NPCs share one identity

Fixed in v4.3 — but if you are upgrading, the memory folder may still hold hundreds of files from the buggy build. Clearing NPC_MEMORIES/ gives you a clean slate.

The chatbox looks wrong on my ultrawide

v4.3 corrects for aspect ratio automatically. If it still feels off, try another Anchor and Scale in the Interface menu, or set UiEngineV2=false to fall back to the legacy renderer.

Race or scene feels weird

Pull the session logs around the exact moment of the issue. v4.3 logs expose the scene, Episode, action, audio, and ownership flow in much greater detail.
